# TCC Hackathon 2026 — Rules, Scoring and Delivery Requirements

**Source:** `TCC Hackathon 2026 — Participant Guide.pdf`, participant guide dated November 15, 2026. The guide is the authority for competition requirements.

## 1. Competition theme

- Conference: Tech Conference Calabar 2026.
- Conference theme: “AI: The New Normal”.
- Hackathon theme: “Solve Local Problems with Global Solutions”.
- Event day: Saturday, November 28, 2026, at The Big Tent, Calabar.
- Build sprint: November 22–24, 2026.

## 2. Dates and hard deadlines

| Date | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Sun, Nov 22, 12:00 AM | Official build sprint begins |
| Sun, Nov 22, 12:00 noon | Registration closes |
| Tue, Nov 24, 11:59 PM WAT | Submission deadline; late entries are not forwarded |
| Wed, Nov 25 | Initial judging; Top 10 announced in WhatsApp group |
| Thu, Nov 26, 7:00 PM | Top 10 virtual semi-final; judges select Top 5 |
| Fri, Nov 27, 9:00 AM | Top 5 announced; official pages reshare videos |
| Sat, Nov 28, 12:00 noon WAT | Social engagement counting closes |
| Sat, Nov 28 | Top 5 present live at The Big Tent; Top 3 awarded |

At least one team member must be physically present at the event on November 28. Top 5 teams present on stage for five minutes each. The guide explicitly says the event-day presentation is a showcase and does not alter the score.

## 3. Eligibility and team rules

- Team size: 1–3 people; solo entries are allowed.
- Participants must be over 16.
- Existing projects are allowed, but the README must clearly separate features built during November 22–24 from pre-existing work. Judges score only sprint work.
- AI tools are allowed and carry no penalty. Disclose each AI tool and what it was used for in the README.
- One entry per team. Only the first submission counts; agree who will submit.

## 4. Required submission

### A. Public demo video

- Exact acceptable duration: 4 minutes 50 seconds to 5 minutes 10 seconds.
- Show the real working product. Slides/mockups alone are not sufficient.
- Suggested video flow: problem and affected users (~30 seconds), working product walkthrough (~4 minutes), technology stack and what comes next (~30 seconds).
- Publish publicly on YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok.
- Submit the public post URL. Private/unlisted videos and file-sharing links such as Google Drive are not accepted.
- Tag Tech Conference Calabar and use `#CTCHackathon2026` and `#TechConferenceCalabar`.
- Test the post in an incognito/private browser window.
- Do not delete, privatise or re-upload after submission; the guide says such a post scores zero social points.

### B. Public source repository

A public GitHub repository is preferred; GitLab and Bitbucket are accepted. `README.md` must explain:

- What the project does.
- How to run it.
- Features built during the sprint (required if the project existed before).
- AI tools used and how they were used.

### C. Official submission form

The form will be shared in the official WhatsApp group. Double-check all links before submission. One submission per team.

## 5. Scorecard: 100 points

| Criterion | Points | What judges assess | MedFlow evidence to demonstrate |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Working Product & Technical Execution | 25 | Actually works, robust features, handled edge cases, honest demo | Complete patient-to-staff workflow; voice and typed paths; transcript editing; extraction; live queue; human review; audit; failure handling |
| Architecture & Code Quality | 20 | Clean structure, security, sensible data handling, readable code | Clear client/server separation; validated contracts; protected secrets; PostgreSQL constraints and immutability triggers; facility-scoped server authorization; modular services; meaningful tests; clean public repo |
| Scalability & Production Readiness | 15 | Deployed/deployable; credible path from prototype to real users | Deployed web app; facility-scoped data model; clear environment configuration; error handling; documented limitations and future integration path |
| Innovation & Local Relevance | 15 | Fresh approach to a specific Calabar/Cross River/Nigerian problem | Voice-first patient storytelling; Nigerian English/Pidgin transcription testing; evidence-linked extraction; address workflow friction, not generic AI chat |
| Demo Quality & Communication | 10 | Clear five-minute story and product demonstration | Tight narrative; one synthetic patient; show real microphone capture and queue update; explain safety boundary and architecture |
| Social engagement | 15 | Eligible engagement on the one submitted video post | Legitimate coordinated support to the exact submitted post |

## 6. Social engagement scoring

Only the submitted demo-video post counts. Engagement on reposts or duplicate copies does not add points.

| Action on submitted post | Points |
|---|---:|
| Reaction / like | 1 |
| Comment (one per account) | 2 |
| Repost / share | 3 |

| Total engagement points | Social score |
|---:|---:|
| 0–14 | 0 |
| 15–29 | 5 |
| 30–49 | 10 |
| 50+ | 15 |

Purchased likes, bots and spam comments are discounted. Clear evidence of bought engagement forfeits the entire social score. Rally legitimate supporters to the single submitted post as soon as it is public. Counting runs until November 28 at 12:00 noon WAT. The guide says the social score goes to the Top 5; every team should build legitimate support from video publication.

## 7. Competition progression

1. All submissions are judged November 25; Top 10 advance.
2. Top 10 present live to judges in the November 26 virtual semi-final; judges select Top 5.
3. Top 5 announced November 27 at 9:00 AM.
4. Top 5 show their product on stage November 28; this is a showcase and does not change scores.
5. Final ranking is based on judge scores (85%) and social engagement (15%). A tie is resolved by judges' deliberation.
